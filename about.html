Our History

Founded in 2008 in Ekalakala, Masinga

St. benedict junior secondary was established in 2008 by a group of educationists who shared a vision of providing quality, affordable education to families in kenya. Starting with just 45 students and 4 teachers, the school has grown to over 850 students and 32 qualified teachers.

Located in Ekalakala masinga subcounty in machakos county, our school has expanded over the years to include modern science laboratories, a computer lab, a well-stocked library, a spacious dining hall, and sports facilities. We offer both the CBC curriculum leading to KJSEA for Seniorsecondary.

Our consistent KJSEA performance — with a mean grade of B+ in 2025 — places us among the top schools in Kenya. We are proud of our alumni who have joined national highschools across Kenya and beyond.
