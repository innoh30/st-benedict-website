Our Curriculum

CBC & 8-4-4 / KJSEA

St. Benedict primary school offers the Competency-Based Curriculum (CBC) for our primary school (Grade 1 to Grade 6) and the junior secondarysystem leading to the Kenya Certificate of Junior Secondary Education (KJSEA) for our secondary school (grade 7 to grade 8).

Our experienced teachers ensure that every learner receives personalised attention. We maintain small class sizes of no more than 30 students per stream, allowing teachers to identify and nurture each child's strengths.

We integrate technology in learning through our computer lab, and our science labs provide hands-on practical experience that prepares students for national examinations and future careers.
