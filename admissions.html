Admission Process

Three simple steps to enrol your child at St. Benedict Junior secondary school.

1

Fill the Online Form

Complete the application form below with your child's details and your contact information.

2

Attend Interview

Our team will call you within 24 hours to schedule an interview and assessment for your child.

3

Pay Admission Fee

Upon successful interview, pay the one-time admission fee of 5,000 KES to secure the place.
