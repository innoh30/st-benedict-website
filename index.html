Welcome Message

A Warm Welcome from Our Director

Fr. Joseph Mutuse

Welcome to St. Benedict Junior secondary school, where we believe every child has the potential to excel. For over 17 years, we have nurtured young minds in Matuu, combining academic rigour with strong Christian values and discipline.

Our committed teachers, modern facilities, and proven track record of KJSEA excellence make us one of the leading schools in the Eastern region Region. We invite you to join our family and experience education that transforms.

Fr. Joseph Mutuse
Director · B.Ed, M.Ed (Kenyatta University)
